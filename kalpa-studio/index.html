FAQ

Common
questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Ask us directly.

How long does a project take?
+
Timelines depend on scope. A landing page takes 5–7 days; a full brand identity 2–3 weeks; a complete website design 3–5 weeks. We'll give you a precise estimate after discovery.
Do you work with startups?
+
Absolutely. We love early-stage companies. We offer startup-friendly packages and flexible payment plans so that budget is never a barrier to great design.
What's included in the handoff?
+
You'll receive source files (Figma, AI, PDF), exported assets in all necessary formats, a usage guide, and a 30-day post-delivery support window for any tweaks.
How many revisions are included?
+
Unlimited rounds within the project scope. We iterate until you're genuinely happy — not just satisfied. We don't count revisions; we count on results.
Do you do development as well?
+
We specialise in design but work closely with trusted development partners. We can manage the full production pipeline if needed — just ask during discovery.
