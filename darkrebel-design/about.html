Our story

We never dreamed
about success.
We worked for it.

Founded in 2020, Kalpa Studio started as a one-person operation with a single belief: that small brands deserve world-class design. Today we're a distributed team of designers, strategists and storytellers serving clients across 20+ countries.

Every project begins with a question: what should people feel when they encounter this brand? The answer shapes everything that follows — from logo to layout, from typeface to tone of voice.
